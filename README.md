### 📌 Course Overview
**MIS443: Business Data Management** is an interdisciplinary course at the intersection of Data Science, Information Systems, and Business Strategy. It provides a comprehensive perspective on how modern organizations capture, store, integrate, and transform raw data into actionable insights to drive strategic decision-making.

### 📚 What I Am Learning
Throughout this course, I am actively developing my skills and exploring key data management domain areas:

* **Core Concepts & Frameworks:** Studying the foundational methodologies behind Databases, Data Warehouses, and Decision Support Systems (DSS).
* **ETL Processes:** Learning to apply Extract, Transform, and Load (ETL) techniques across various business domains to ensure data integrity and seamless integration.
* **Data Tools & Practical Application:** Utilizing **SQL** and **Python** to query, clean, transform, and derive insights from real-world business datasets.
* **Applied Problem-Solving & Data Ethics:** Engaging in practical, problem-based scenarios and case studies to strengthen my analytical mindset while learning to handle data responsibly and ethically.
